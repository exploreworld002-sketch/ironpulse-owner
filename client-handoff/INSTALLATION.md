# Ironpulse — client installation

Your vendor supplies the Windows installer for your computer. This document
alone is not the app. You do not need Node.js, MongoDB, Docker, or terminal commands.

## Windows — unsigned release

1. Download `Ironpulse-Setup-x64.exe` directly from your vendor.
2. Double-click the installer. Windows may show **Unknown publisher** or
   **Windows protected your PC** because this release does not have a code-signing
   certificate. This is expected for this unsigned release; it is not a verification
   that the file is safe.
3. If the file came directly from your vendor and you have confirmed it is the
   intended release, click **More info → Run anyway** when that option is available.
   If you are unsure about the download, contact your vendor first.
4. Installation runs for your Windows account and opens Ironpulse. Afterwards use
   the Ironpulse desktop shortcut or Start menu entry.

If your computer does not offer **Run anyway**, contact your vendor or IT support.
Do not disable Defender, Smart App Control, or your organization's security policy.
This installer does not need a separate database, Node.js, Docker or terminal setup.

Support: contact the vendor who supplied your installer and activation license.

## First activation

1. Click **Save activation request** and send the saved file to your vendor.
2. Save the `.ironlicense` file they send back. In Ironpulse click **Import license
   file** and select it.
3. Create your own administrator email and password (12 or more characters).
4. Sign in, create membership plans, then add members and record payments.

There are no demo records or default login credentials. Normal launches after setup
open your existing local workspace. Activation needs the vendor's response; it is
not automatic online activation. A replacement computer or Windows reinstall needs
vendor reactivation. Keep your computer clock correct.

## Daily use

- Payments → Payment method: select All, Cash, UPI, Card or Bank Transfer to see
  matching payments and total collections. Dashboard and Revenue Reports have the
  same filter. Unpaid dues are independent of payment method.
- Memberships → Edit changes the plan for future enrollments/renewals. Existing
  members keep their agreed fees and history.
- Settings → Download backup saves business data and member photos. Store a copy on
  another drive regularly. Restore replaces business records and requires Admin.
- Receipt/report → Print opens your OS print dialog; choose Save as PDF if required.
- SMS/WhatsApp/email reminders are message previews only; they do not send messages.

Keep your administrator password and backups safe. Records are stored on this
computer, not automatically synchronized to a cloud service or another computer.
Contact your vendor before replacing hardware, resetting Windows, or moving data.
